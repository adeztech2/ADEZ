M-Pesa Deposit

Enter the amount you want to deposit and your M-Pesa phone number. Payment confirmation will be handled by the secure payment backend.

Never enter your M-Pesa PIN, password, or OTP on the ADEZ TECH website. Those credentials should only be entered into the official M-Pesa payment interface when prompted.

How Deposits Work

1. Enter the amount you want to add.

2. Enter the M-Pesa number that should receive the payment request.

3. Continue to the secure payment process.

4. After the payment provider confirms payment, your ADEZ TECH wallet will be credited.

5. Use your wallet balance to pay for available services.

🔒 Payment Security

Your wallet should only be credited after the payment provider sends a verified confirmation to the ADEZ TECH server.
